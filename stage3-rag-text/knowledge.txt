Project Nebula is an internal initiative at Apex Dynamics launched in June 2025.
The project lead for Nebula is Dr. Sarah Lin, and the chief systems architect is Marcus Vance.
The primary goal of Project Nebula is to reduce latency in microservice meshes by 42% using eBPF probes.
Nebula operates on a quarterly budget of $350,000 and is slated for general production rollout in October 2026.
If an outage occurs within Nebula, emergency tickets must be assigned the tag #NEBULA-RED and escalated directly to Marcus Vance within 15 minutes.
